! Title: Smart TV Tracking & Telemetry Blocklist
! Description: Blocks ACR (Automatic Content Recognition), ad, and telemetry domains used by popular smart TV platforms (LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, Google TV/Android TV, Roku OS, Vizio).
! Version: 2026.10.28
! Last modified: 2026-10-28
!
! USAGE
!   Paste into AdGuard Home -> Filters -> Custom filtering rules,
!   or save as a .txt file and add under DNS blocklists.
!   "||domain^" blocks the domain and all of its subdomains.
!
! NOTES
!   - Domains are compiled from community lists, forum reports and
!     RTINGS smart TV tracking tests (July 2026). They are not official
!     and manufacturers change endpoints regularly.
!   - Blocking DNS does not replace disabling ACR in the TV's settings.
!   - Some TVs use hardcoded DNS servers or DoH and bypass AdGuard Home.
!     Redirect port 53 from the TV to AdGuard Home on your router and
!     block port 853 (DoT) and known DoH IPs for the TV.
!   - If an app or feature breaks, check the Query Log and add an
!     exception, e.g. @@||example.com^
